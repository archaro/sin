Bytecode layout:

Byte  Size  Usage
0     1     How many locals are in use?
1     1     Of which locals, how many are parameters?
2     end   Bytecode for interpreter to run.

IR encoding note:
- Opcode 'F' is intentionally shared by IR_OP_ITEM_DEREF and IR_OP_CALL.
  Both operations execute the same VM primitive (fetch item contents).
  CALL encodes one extra immediate byte (argc); ITEM_DEREF does not.
